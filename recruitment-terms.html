2. Registering your interest

Registration tells us you would like to be considered for suitable opportunities. It does not guarantee an interview, a vacancy or an offer of employment. We assess registrations against relevant role requirements and contact candidates where there is a suitable match. Roles and recruitment needs may change, and a role description does not guarantee an immediate vacancy.

3. Location and employment

The opportunities promoted on this website are office-based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Working with UK clients does not mean employment in the UK, relocation to the UK or UK visa sponsorship. The employing business, work location, hours, pay and any conditions of an offer will be set out in writing before you accept employment.

4. Your information and assessments

Please provide accurate, up-to-date information about your qualifications, experience and skills, and upload only documents you are entitled to share. Tell us if important details change. Recruitment may include interviews, practical assessments and relevant checks, which we will explain when applicable. An IELTS result is optional; you can register without one.

5. Development and study support

We aim to support professional development, including accountancy studies and English communication skills. The support available will be discussed according to your role and development needs. Any agreed funding, study leave or other specific support will be confirmed separately in writing. Website descriptions do not guarantee particular funding, qualifications or promotion.

6. Recruitment fees

We do not charge candidates to register, attend interviews or receive an offer of employment. If anyone requests a recruitment payment in our name, do not pay and contact info@outsourcesolutionsbd.com.

7. Privacy and group opportunities

Our separate Privacy Policy explains how we handle your registration and CV, including recruitment-related sharing with authorised personnel across companies and brands within our group. It also explains how to withdraw consent or request deletion. These terms do not give permission for unrelated uses of your information.

8. Questions and changes

You can withdraw from recruitment or ask questions by emailing info@outsourcesolutionsbd.com. We may update these terms as our recruitment process changes; the date above shows the latest version. Changes to these website terms do not amend an employment agreement or remove any rights you have under applicable law.
