FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Register your interest and we'll be in touch if there's a suitable role.

Is registering the same as applying for a job?
No. Registering tells us you're interested in working with us. We review every registration against our open roles and contact you if there's a suitable match.
Do I have to pay anything to register or be hired?
No. We never charge fees at any stage of our recruitment. If anyone asks you for money in our name, don't pay and treat it as a scam.
Are these roles remote?
No. All roles are office-based at our Dhaka office.
Do I need to be ACCA, ACA or CIMA qualified?
Not for every role. Requirements vary by role, and you can register if you're part-qualified or planning to start your studies.
Do I need an IELTS score?
No. An IELTS score is optional. If you have one, it helps us understand your English level.
